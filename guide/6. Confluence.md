Work on Confluence pages as normal notes, then send your edits back.

## Fetch

- Click **+** next to **Confluence** in the sidebar, paste a page link, tick the pages you want and **Fetch**. Sub-pages become notebooks.
- The first time, a browser tab asks you to sign in to Atlassian — click **Accept**.
- Parts Markdown can't show (macros, layouts, special tables) appear as 🔒 boxes. They go back unchanged.
- The **Confluence** section in the sidebar shows the page tree like Confluence: every page, in order. Pages with sub-pages have a ▸; clicking one opens that page.

## Refresh (get other people's changes and new pages)

- Right-click **All pages** → **Refresh All from Confluence…** (or right-click any Confluence folder → **Refresh from Confluence…**, or ⌘K → *Refresh Confluence Pages*).
- The Fetch window opens with that page tree loaded: pages you already have say *already here, will update*, new ones are added. Click **Fetch**.
- Pages you edited but haven't pushed are skipped, so your edits are never overwritten — push them first (see **Changes to push** below).
- For a single page: ⌘K → **Pull Latest from Confluence**.
- **Pictures:** click **Show pictures from Confluence** in a 🔒 box to see images and draw.io diagrams (one more sign-in the first time).

## Push your edits

- Edit the note, then **Push**. Only the parts you changed are updated. Choose **Publish** or **Save as draft**.
- If someone changed the page meanwhile, Push stops instead of overwriting it. Use **Pull Latest from Confluence** (⌘K) to refresh.
- **Changes to push** (in the sidebar's Confluence section, and an orange **edited** label in the list) lists every page you edited but haven't pushed: **Show changes**, **Push** one, or **Push all**.

## Publish a note as a new page

- On a normal note, click **Push** and choose where it goes (a page or folder you fetched, or paste a link) — or drag the note onto a page or folder in the Confluence section.
- After **Publish** the note moves into your Confluence notebooks and stays linked.

## Good to know

- On a company network that inspects secure connections, VibeNote may not be able to reach Atlassian — it says so. Use another network (home Wi-Fi, a phone hotspot) or ask IT to install the network's certificate on your Mac.
- Pictures stored only on your Mac can't be uploaded to Confluence yet; Push tells you when one is left out.
