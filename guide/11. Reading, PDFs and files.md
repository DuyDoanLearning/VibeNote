VibeNote can be your reading desk: save articles, keep PDFs, Word and Excel files next to your notes, and highlight as you read.

## Save a web page

- **⇧⌘L** (or ⌘K → **Save Web Page**): paste a link and press Return.
- An **article** becomes a clean note (no menus or ads) with the site, author and dates at the top. It goes into the **Reading** notebook, or into the notebook you have open. Formulas come along as real math.
- A link to a **PDF** saves the PDF itself.
- Pages that need a sign-in, or that are built entirely by scripts, may not save. VibeNote says so.

## Highlight while you read

- In the **preview**, select text and click **Highlight**. It becomes `==highlighted==` in the note. Select inside a highlight to **Remove** it.
- In the editor, **⇧⌘H** does the same.
- ⌘K → **Highlights** shows every highlight in all your notes, with a filter. Click one to jump there.

## PDF, Word and Excel files

- **Add Files…** (⌘K or the File menu), or drag files from Finder onto the note list. They are copied into the notebook; your originals stay where they were.
- They appear in the list with a **PDF**, **Word** or **Excel** label, and you can move, rename and delete them like notes.
- **PDF:** opens in a full PDF viewer: search, thumbnails, zoom, and tools to **highlight**, draw, add text and comments (top right). Your marks are saved into the PDF by themselves, so Preview and other apps see them too. The very first original is kept safe in VibeNote's settings folder.
- **Word:** read it inside VibeNote. **Make a note from it** turns it into a normal note you can highlight and edit.
- **Excel:** every sheet as a table. **Make a note from this sheet** turns the sheet into a table in a note.
- ⋯ → **Open in Preview / Word / Excel** opens the real app. Editing a Word or Excel file happens there.
